Wireframe Project

I will always cherish my first freelance project because it marks the beginning of my career. Through this experience, I learned new skills, adapted my UX knowledge to fresh ideas, and grew as a designer while collaborating with a client. I am grateful to have had this opportunity.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Wireframing
  • HTML and CSS Coding
  • Web Design

Programs Used

  • Figma
  • Sublime Text

Planning

When first discussing what Christopher wanted from his portfolio, he gave me a list of his peer’s official webpages to gather inspiration from. Included with this list were elements he wanted to see on his official site. These elements being:

  • Having his more notable roles to be displayed in the forefront.
  • Having a notable headshot.
  • Including links for his social media, IMDB resume, and convention booking agent.
  • Including an introduction paragraph about himself.

With these necessities in mind, I started drafting in Figma to showcase these key aspects.

Drafting

Since there was some inspiration to look at, and no clear visual direction given from the client, I decided to make two drafts in Figma to present to him as a starting point. I treated these as something visual to show the client so he can get a better understanding of my thought process.

Draft 1

For the first draft, I wanted to focus on a one-page portfolio as seen in Aaron Campbell and Mauricio Ortiz’s sites. I wanted it to have a more modern look with minimal color and mostly whitespace to emphasize the graphics on the page. I wanted to keep and emphasis on his wants for the site, so I treated this as a simple solution to his problems.

Draft 2

For the next draft, I didn’t want to stray too far from the original design principles I used in the first. Rather, I wanted to switch the color story of the site by giving it a more graphic feel. Additionally, unlike the first draft, I wanted to separate the pages on the site to make it feel more familiar as a common webpage. I wanted the font to pop more against the color and made them wider to give it a more graphic feel.

Critique

After presenting these drafts to the client, he informed me of some minor changes that he wanted to see. Just minor grammar changes, but he seemed happy with the first drafts. After fixing these errors, I could start coding the final product.

Coding

To code the site, I utilized Sublime Text as my editor. Using HTML5 and CSS to design the site and GitHub to host, I was able to bring the project to life. Additionally, I was able to learn new techniques such as:

  • How to stylize mp4 files.
  • How to implement media inquiries for mobile accessibility.
  • How to code for responsive containers.

After releasing the official website, I neglected to test it on mobile viewing and found that all my current code broke. I had to regrettably inform my client that I had to implement more coding for the site to be fully launched. It was an oversight on my part but taught me a valuable lesson in being thorough with testing in the future.
